# Calculation Verification

## Purpose

Reduce errors in arithmetic, algebra, unit conversion, formulas, statistics, and quantitative reasoning.

## Workflow

1. Define the problem
   - What is being solved?
   - What are the inputs?
   - What are the unknowns?
   - What units apply?

2. Choose the method
   - Identify the formula, equation, algorithm, or statistical method.
   - Explain why it applies when there may be alternatives.

3. Estimate first
   - Do a rough magnitude check before precise calculation.
   - Predict sign, range, and plausible units.

4. Calculate
   - Substitute values carefully.
   - Keep units through the calculation.
   - Avoid early rounding.
   - Use a reliable computational tool for multi-step or error-prone work.

5. Verify
   - Recalculate by another method when feasible.
   - Check limiting cases.
   - Check unit compatibility.
   - Check sign, decimal placement, and magnitude.
   - Confirm significant figures or precision requirements.

6. Present
   - Give the answer first.
   - Include units.
   - Show only essential working unless detailed working is requested.
   - State uncertainty if inputs are approximate.

## Error checklist

Watch for:
- percentage vs decimal,
- degrees vs radians,
- reciprocal errors,
- missing square/cube,
- metric prefixes,
- sign errors,
- logarithm base errors,
- rounding too early,
- inconsistent units,
- hidden assumptions in averages or rates.
